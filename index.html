About Higher End.
Brandon Lozano Martin
Founder, Higher End Denver

Seven years ago, Brandon Lozano Martin wasn't running events for clients, he was running them for a cause. Leading a nonprofit taught him something most event planners never learn: what it truly feels like when an event matters. When the details are right, people don't just attend, they remember.

But he kept seeing the same thing from other companies. Big promises. Short delivery. Clients left wondering where their money went and why the night felt hollow.

So he built Higher End.

The standard is simple: true to price, true to promise, and true to the moment. In our first year, we've already executed 8 events and earned perfect feedback across every single one, because we don't just coordinate logistics, we build stories.

That commitment showed up loudly at our Chanukah celebration at the Capital, a 300-person event where the Governor came and delivered remarks. Not because it was easy. Because every detail was earned.

When you book Higher End, you're not hiring a vendor. You're bringing in a team that treats your event like it's the only one that matters, because on that day, it is.
